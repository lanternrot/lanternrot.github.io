Studio

Made with AI

Lantern Rot is one person and a lot of AI. I decide what gets made, how it should play, and when it's ready. AI does most of the making.

  • Code is written by AI coding agents (Anthropic's Claude). I direct the work, sign off on what each feature has to do, and play every build before it ships.
  • Art in Dorf (heroes, monsters, maps, backgrounds, icons) is generated with Google Gemini and animated with PixelLab. Most of the art in Cat Cafe, Duckie Pond, Cookie Cafe and Fox Den is generated with Gemini. I pick, re-prompt and reject, and scripts clean up and resize what stays.
  • Wizard's Grimoire draws its book in code, to a look designed with the streamer it was made for. Its entries are drafted by AI and fact-checked by a second AI.
  • The lantern logo started as Gemini drafts and was rebuilt as exact shapes in code.
  • Music in Dorf is made with Suno.
  • Sound effects in Dorf are a mix of AI-generated sounds and free recordings from Freesound. I pick each one.
  • Words in Dorf (hero lines, tales) and the news posts on this site are drafted by AI. I read every news post before it goes up.

All of this happens while the games are being made. Nothing in Dorf or the chat games runs an AI model while you play.

Why say so? Some people would rather not play things made this way, or put them on their stream. That's a fair call, and you should get to make it before you install anything or ask me to build something.
