Studio

Made with AI

Lantern Rot is one person and a lot of AI. I decide what gets made, how it should play, and when it's ready. AI does most of the making.

That covers nearly every part of a project: the code, the art and animation, the music and sound, and much of the writing. I direct the work, keep what's good, throw out what isn't, and play or use everything before it ships.

The tools change as better ones come along and as each project needs something different, so I don't list them here. I'm always happy to talk shop, though.

Most of the AI work happens while something is being made. If a game or tool uses AI while it runs (to write a line on the spot, say), its own page says so.

Why say so? Some people would rather not play things made this way, or put them on their stream. That's a fair call, and you should get to make it before you install anything or ask me to build something.
