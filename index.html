Twitch chat games

Games that live in a streamer's chat: viewers type commands, collect things, and watch it happen on stream. Each one is built for a specific channel.

Cat Cafe

Viewers lure and care for wandering cats during the stream. At the end, the winner adopts one for keeps.

Duckie Pond

Chat adopts ducks with !bread. Ducks stay between streams and nap while the channel is offline.

Cookie Cafe

Every chatter gets a fox kit that digs for seasonal finds.
