Our game

Built for streamers

Twitch chat games

Games that live in a streamer's chat: viewers type commands, collect things, and watch it happen on stream. Each one is built for a specific streamer's channel.

Cat Cafe

Viewers lure and care for wandering cats during the stream. At the end, the winner adopts one for keeps.

Duckie Pond

Chat adopts ducks with !bread. Ducks stay between streams and nap while the channel is offline. 162 looks to discover.

Cookie Cafe

Chat bakes and collects cookies: 120 to find across four rarity tiers, kept forever in a CookieDex.

Fox Den

Every chatter gets a fox kit that digs for seasonal finds, and the streamer's vixen carries one to her corner of the screen.

Wizard's Grimoire

A channel-point redeem opens a book on stream with a real entry from history, science, philosophy or words. Spell duels are next.
