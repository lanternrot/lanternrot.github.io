The threshold between earth and abyss. The merfolk hold the Gate to Aquaria, and something under the water holds the merfolk. It has eight arms, and it has noticed you.

These notes cover 5.2.0 and 5.2.1, which went out half an hour apart. You can also earn Summon Tickets now by hanging out on our Twitch streams, which is the closest thing to a wage Dorf has ever offered.

## The Gate to Aquaria, rebuilt

The Gate has a new map, a sea cave closed in on every side, with the Kraken's dark bay in its corner. His arms stand in the water beside the places they reach into. At the Held Ship, the galleon still hangs in the air, held up by the arm that took it. Most of the Gate's fights have new waves.

### The Great Kraken Arm

The arm that took the galleon still holds it above the water, and it's twice the girth of its sisters. Cut it and the Kraken takes it badly, so whoever makes the cut had better be ready for him.

> The arm that took the galleon and still holds it above the water. Twice the girth of its sisters, the Kraken flinches violently when it is severed.

## Summon Tickets from our Twitch streams

Link your Twitch account in Dorf and your Channel Points on Lantern Rot streams can buy you Summon Tickets.

Open Settings and find the Twitch section. Tap **Link Twitch** and Dorf shows you a code. Tap **Open Twitch** and approve that code, and Settings says you're linked, with your Twitch name.

Then, on a Lantern Rot stream, redeem the **Daily Gacha Hero Pull** or the **Extra Gacha Hero Pull**. You still get your hero on stream, and a letter holding a Summon Ticket lands in your Dorf mailbox the next time you open the game. The link lives on your phone, not in your save.

## Bloats in the Floodplain

After the rains, nothing stays buried. Some of it comes back up full of gas.

Naga Territory is built around the Bloats now, and Murky Depths, Serpent's Crossing and Peat Cuttings each have one waiting. A mark on a Bloat's plate tells you which kind you're looking at.

## Something in the snow

{% include figure.html src="extra_climber.webp" side=true scale=2 scale_sm=2 alt="The Extra Climber, a pale hooded figure wrapped in snow-white rags, a coil of rope trailing behind it" caption="The Extra Climber" %}

At Guardian's Gate, an Extra Climber stalks you from the snow. You can't pick it out to strike it, though a blow that sweeps the whole field still finds it. Each turn nobody hits it, it creeps a step closer. On its third step it comes out of the snow and strikes your weakest hero.

It turns up again in the Hall of Winds, the Frost Sanctum and Titan's Throne.

> Count a rope team in a whiteout and you come up one too many. Nobody remembers tying it on, and nobody sees it come.

## The Embalmer

{% include figure.html src="embalmer.webp" side=true scale=2 scale_sm=2 alt="The Embalmer, a gaunt man in a stained leather apron, holding a needle and thread, a lantern at his belt" caption="The Embalmer" %}

The Lich has hired help. In the Hall of Bones, an Embalmer evens out the wounds of the dead around it on its turn, so the foe you nearly finished gets patched up from the healthy ones. Kill it first, or finish your target before its turn comes round.

It turns up again in the Wraith Sanctum, the Ritual Chamber and the Chapel of Candles.

> A living man in the Lich's pay, who keeps the dead presentable. He goes among them with needle and waxed thread, taking a little from the whole to mend the torn, until no one of them looks worse than the rest.

## Battles are heard

Battles have 21 new sounds. A tap on a target answers you, and a tap on one you can't pick tells you so. A hero's turn coming up sets their book down, and an enemy's scuffs a boot. The skill book riffles open, flips its pages and shuts.

War drums beat as a new wave walks in, and a sword goes back in its sheath when a wave falls and another is on its way. Buffs and debuffs make a sound as they run out, an enemy winding up a big blow draws its weapon back, and you'll hear it when a boss changes phase.

After a win, gold and gems count up with a coin tick and a stone tick, and the purse lands on the last one. With Reduce Motion on, the totals show at once.
