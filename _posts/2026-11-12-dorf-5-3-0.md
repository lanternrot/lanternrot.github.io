Colours unseen by surface eyes bloom in the dark. Past the Gate, the reef runs right up to Aquaria's walls, and everything living on it bites. Every bite takes a little piece of you, and the piece stays gone until the wave is over.

Under the reef, the city keeps its prison. Kill a guard down there and somebody picks up his keys. Somebody always does. At the bottom of it all, a smith in one of the cells would like a word with your gear.

## The Coral Depths, rebuilt

The Coral Depths have a new map: the reef on the sea floor outside Aquaria's walls. It's alive and glowing at the way in, where shafts of light still come down through the water, and it's bleached white by the time you reach the city. Bubbles rise off the rocks, and jellyfish drift over the reef. The road runs eleven fights now, five of them new.

Everything with teeth down here takes a piece of whoever it bites. A bite lowers that hero's max HP for the rest of the wave, and no heal reaches past it. When the wave ends, every hero is whole again.

## The Drowned Prison, rebuilt

{% include figure.html src="prison_inmate.webp" side=true scale=2 scale_sm=2 alt="An Inmate, a grey-skinned prisoner in rags and iron cuffs, a shiv in one hand" caption="An Inmate" %}

Inmates and wardens alike never left. The Drowned Prison is down a grate in Eel Hollows, and it runs on one house rule. When a guard falls, the next inmate to act picks up his keys and becomes him. It keeps its own body and whatever HP it had, and takes up the job right there. A key on a plate shows who holds a post.

> Nobody remembers what it was sent down for, least of all itself. It has been down here long enough to know where the keys hang.

Kill the Warden and you'll be fighting him again a moment later, in somebody else's body.

Chained inmates do nothing at all. A Chain Golem holds a whole line of them, and its chains carry more than weight. Once they're loose, inmates fight as a crowd, and a crowd makes each of them braver. The Taskmaster tells the crowd who to go for, and the Drowner waits for anyone who can't move.

{% include figure.html src="prison_06.webp" side=true alt="The Ducking Stools: a stone cell block under water, a wooden chair on a long beam over a dark pool, a lantern on a pillar and cell bars along the wall" caption="The Ducking Stools" %}

The road runs six fights now. The Ducking Stools are new, just before the Warden's Sanctum. That's where the jail puts one prisoner through the whole routine.

## Halia and the Blacksmith's gear

Clear the Warden's Sanctum and you free Halia, a merfolk smith the kingdom locked away. She builds weapons, armor, rings and cloaks at the Blacksmith on Market Street, and raises each one a star at a time with iron and gold.

### How gear works now

You build a piece once, then raise that same piece in place. It stays on its wearer as it goes up a star and takes the new star's name. Before, pieces only dropped, and you raised one by melting two copies of it together.

A piece now gives a share of its wearer's own stats, counted before gear, with level, stars and attunement all included. So a piece keeps its worth as the hero levels. Here is each piece from 1★ to 5★:

| Piece | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon | +5, +12, +25, +45, +80 ATK | 3%, 6%, 10%, 15%, 20% of ATK |
| Armor | +3, +8, +15, +28, +50 DEF and +20, +50, +100, +180, +300 HP | 2%, 4%, 7%, 10%, 14% of DEF, and the same of HP |
| Ring | +2, +5, +10, +18, +30 ATK and DEF | 1%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 7% of ATK, and the same of DEF |
| Cloak | +3, +6, +12, +20, +35 SPD | +2, +4, +6, +8, +10 SPD |

**Why:** Gear gave flat numbers, and on the new level curve they faded fast. A 5★ weapon added about 28% ATK to a damage dealer at level 50, about 5% at level 95, and next to nothing by level 225. A share keeps its worth at every level. SPD doesn't grow with level, so the cloak stays flat, but the old numbers could let a 5★ cloak more than double a hero's speed.
{: .why}

A ring's or cloak's effect starts at 3★ now. The 3★ to 5★ effects are unchanged.

| Piece | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper Charm (2★ ring) | +2 MP a turn | No effect |
| Traveler's Cape (2★ cloak) | Recovers 5 MP as each new wave starts | No effect |

**Why:** Ring and cloak effects are still being rebalanced. The effects at 1★ and 2★ will get another look later.
{: .why}

Class items haven't changed, and they can't be built or raised.

### What it costs

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.irons scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

| Step | Iron | Gold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build a 1★ piece | 3 Scrap Iron | 7,000 |
| 1★ to 2★ | 5 Pig Iron | 12,000 |
| 2★ to 3★ | 6 Steel Ingot | 35,000 |
| 3★ to 4★ | 8 Blackiron Ingot | 175,000 |
| 4★ to 5★ | 10 Starfall Iron | 750,000 |

Nothing drops Starfall Iron yet.

**Why:** Before, only the twelve 1★ pieces ever dropped. A 5★ piece took sixteen drops of that one piece, and the rarer materials dropped nowhere, so nothing could get past 3★. Now one iron a star serves every piece, and you pick which favourite gets better.
{: .why}

### Where the iron comes from

Once Halia is free:

- Every region fight on Hard, and every Normal fight in Aquarias, drops iron. Quest Token quick-clears count too. The higher the fight's level, the better the iron: Scrap Iron at any level, Pig Iron from level 67, Steel Ingots from level 95 and Blackiron Ingots from level 140.
- The General Store sells 3 Scrap Iron a day, at 500 gold each.
- The Gem Merchant's daily deals can offer any iron your won fights drop.
- The daily parcel gives 2 Scrap Iron on days 16 and 18, and a Pig Iron on day 20.

### Your gear comes with you

Every weapon, armor, ring and cloak you own becomes a built piece at its star, still on the hero who wore it. Each old upgrade material becomes one iron of its tier: common to Scrap Iron, uncommon to Pig Iron, rare to Steel Ingot and epic to Blackiron Ingot. Your save is backed up on the device before the change.
