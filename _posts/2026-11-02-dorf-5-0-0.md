Ancient trees whisper warnings to those who dare enter. For a long time the warning should have been about levels. Once a hero passed level 10, each level added about 1% to their stats, so you could climb for a week and hit about as hard as when you started. The fights kept getting harder anyway.

Dorf 5.0 rebuilds the climb from the first fight up. Every level makes a hero stronger now, all the way to 250, and every fight in the world has a level of its own to meet you at.

## A new level curve

### What was wrong

A level did very little after level 10. Each one added about 1% to a hero's HP, ATK and DEF. A whole region's worth of levels added about 5%, which was less than one fusion star. You could feel a new skill or a new hero. You couldn't feel a level.

That one small number caused bigger trouble. A level couldn't make a hero stronger, so it couldn't tell you whether you were ready for a fight either. And the rewards had drifted away from it. From the Stormwind Peaks on, every replay paid the full 100-gem cap, and two hours of play a day earned four to six times the XP of half an hour. There was no way to tune a fight for both players at once.

### What a level does now

Every level makes a hero about 4% stronger. It's 4.4% a level up to level 12, then 3.7% a level all the way to 250. Each level builds on the last, and it's the same at every star. Five levels now add about 20%, twice what a fusion star gives.

| Level | ATK before | ATK now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 159 | 160 |
| 45 | 220 | 531 |
| 95 | 361 | 3,264 |
| 145 | 590 | 20,077 |
| 250 | 1,656 | 910,886 |

That's a hero with 100 base ATK. The before column is a 3★ on the old curve, and each star grew a little differently. HP and DEF climb the same way.

The monsters climb the same curve. Every fight has a level now, and its monsters are built for that level, so what counts is your level against the fight's. Every fight in a region shares one level, so a monster is the same monster at every stop in its region.

The numbers get big. Late in the game, hits land in the hundreds of thousands and bosses carry billions of HP. Damage floaters, HP bars and counters show them short (12.3k), and the battle log and the hero page keep the full number.

**DEF keeps its worth.** It used to count for more the higher both sides got. Now it's measured against the fight's level, so DEF does as much at level 200 as at level 5.

**SPD and MP stay put.** A hero's SPD and MP are the same at every level, 1.9 times their base, with a little more for each star gained. Turn order no longer shifts as heroes level, and extra speed comes from gear and buffs.

### Your roster climbs together

From level 12, your sixth-best hero sets the level for everyone. Every hero below it is lifted up to it, and a newly summoned hero joins at it. Your best heroes can climb up to five levels past it. The floor used to sit five levels under your sixth-best hero. Now it is that hero's level.

### Attunement

Attunement comes from levels now. A hero earns a point every 10 levels from level 10 to level 200, 20 in all. Each point adds 1% of one base stat, and one stat can take up to 15. Every hero's first reset is free. Attunement Runes retire.

### Fusion

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each star gained | +15% HP, ATK and DEF | +10% HP, ATK and DEF |

**Why:** With levels doing more of the work, heroes fused up from 1★ were ending up with more stats than heroes born at 5★.
{: .why}

### Rewards on the curve

- **Fights pay XP and gold by their level.** A replay at or above your level pays in full, and the map marks it "At your level". A replay below your level pays less the further behind you it sits. A first clear always pays in full.
- **Gems.** A replay at your level pays 16 gems (4 in the Whispering Woods, the Echoing Caverns and the Stormwind Peaks), then 12, 8 and 4 for one, two and three levels behind, and none past that. First clears pay their gems as before. That is a big cut from the 100 that late replays paid. Gems were coming in faster than they meant anything.
- **One Quest Token** quick-clears any fight you've cleared, at a replay's XP and gold. Two come in every daily parcel.
- **The daily parcel** grows with the calendar. Each 28-day round pays more gold and XP than the last, plus 3,450 gems, four Summon Tickets and a Grand Summon Ticket. A new game's first three parcels each add a free ten-pull.
- **The Library's book shelf** has ten tiers that open as you progress, with the Ancient Codex from the Stormwind Peaks.
- **The tavern** pays by the furthest region you've reached.

## What's next: skill upgrades

Levels now carry a hero's raw strength, which leaves room for something we've wanted for a while. Next, a hero's skills get better on a track of their own, with materials you farm from the Genus Loci and the Maw. Hero shards retire in this update to make way for it, and every shard you earned comes back as gold.

## Your save comes with you

Your save converts the first time you open 5.0. The game looks at the furthest fight you've won and sets your roster's level from it.

- **Nobody loses a level.** Heroes below the new level are lifted to it, and your best six can lead it by up to five.
- **XP waiting in the Library** keeps its worth in levels.
- **Attunement starts fresh.** Every point you had spent, and every rune in your bag, comes back as 100 Anima.
- **Region tokens become Quest Tokens**, one for one.
- **Hero shards retire.** Every shard you earned, held or spent, comes back as gold.
- **Colosseum progress** carries over by strength.
- **Expeditions already out** come home when they were due.
- **A copy of your old save** stays on the device as a backup.

### Or start fresh

We'd love it if you started over. The new curve was built from the first fight up, and the first hour is where it's easiest to feel and where your feedback helps us most. A new game also gets a free ten-pull in each of its first three daily parcels.

1. In Dorf, open Settings, then Save, and tap **Export save** to keep a copy of your game.
2. On your phone, open Settings, then Apps, then Dorf, then Storage, and tap **Clear storage** (**Clear data** on some phones).
3. Open Dorf. The intro plays and you start at the edge of the Whispering Woods.

Clearing storage also deletes the backup on the device, so export first if you might want your old game back. **Import save** in the same Settings section brings it back.

Then tell us how it feels.

### A free ten-pull either way

Whichever you choose, a free ten-pull is waiting in your mailbox, by the gear on Home. Thank you for playing.

Every region from the Echoing Caverns to the Gate to Aquaria has been rebuilt. Every fight is four waves, every fight in a region shares one level, and each region has a trick of its own to learn.

## The Colosseum, rebuilt

The Colosseum climbs to 250 bouts, one level each, from bout 1 at level 1 to bout 250 at level 250. The bouts are split across five arenas of 50: the Dregs, the Proving Grounds, Blood & Iron, the Crucible and the Hall of Legends. Your opponents come at more stars as you climb. Each bout is fought once.

Before a bout, the two parties face each other on the arena's painting, leaders at the front. Tap one of theirs to read them before the fight starts. Win, and the crowd throws its laurels.

## Hero changes

### Berserkers

| Rule | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rage from one skill's hits | 10 for each hit that lands | 10 at most, however many hits land |

Hits taken still pay 10 Rage each. Skills that give Rage of their own, like Bale Hook and Hex-Eater, still add it on top.

**Why:** Multi-hit skills were paying for themselves, so a Berserker could cast them over and over without running dry. A cast should cost more than it earns back.
{: .why}

### Darl

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Toad Strangler | 35 Rage. Four hits of 30% ATK, each giving back 10 Rage | 20 Rage. Four hits of 30% ATK |
| Burndown | 65 Rage | 45 Rage |

**Why:** Under the Rage cap his skills cost less, so they come around about as often as before.

## Fixes

- An effect a hero puts on the party or an ally now lasts on the caster as long as its card says. The caster's own copy was running out a turn early.
- The Arbiter's Judicial Precedent card names the three skills that recover MP: Gavel Strike, Sustained Objection and Sealed Evidence.
- The level-up, Rank Up and fusion scenes no longer jump when "Tap to continue" appears.
