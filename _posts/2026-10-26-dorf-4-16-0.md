Old stones stand along the roads of Dorf, and for months they handed out blessings without a word about what any of them did. You knelt, you picked the name that sounded best, and you hoped.

The stones talk now. So does the tavern, and the download got a lot lighter on the way.

## Shrines

Open a shrine and it tells you what it's for. Take one blessing, and it helps every fight in that region for 24 hours, except in Hard mode. Each blessing shows its name, what it does with its icon, and its old line of flavour underneath. The one you have on says how long it has left.

The map tells you too. A shrine's stone is lit while one of its blessings is on and goes dark when none is. In battle, the blessing's badge names the blessing and the shrine it came from.

The Glowstone Altar in the Echoing Caverns also says what it guards: taking a blessing there opens the road to Troll Bridge.

Three of the damage blessings were giving far more than they said. They were meant as a small edge and were adding over double damage, in the Whispering Woods too.

| Blessing | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predator's Instinct (Whispering Woods) | +115% damage | +15% damage |
| Cavern Fury (Echoing Caverns) | +120% damage | +20% damage |
| Avalanche Fury (Stormwind Peaks) | +115% damage | +15% damage |

**Why:** They were written as totals but the game read them as bonuses. These are the numbers they were always meant to be.
{: .why}

## The tavern shows you around

A new player walking into the tavern had no way of knowing the mess could be cleaned, the meal table cooks, or the gaming table has games. Now the party leader speaks up, one thing at a time, the first time each one matters.

> Dice rattle at the gaming table, and Shasha Ember Witch turns to look. Tap the table to see this week's games; the board is wiped and chalked anew every week.
>
> The tavern, with Shasha Ember Witch leading the party

The trash and the gaming die glint, and the meal table steams, until you've used each one once. A hint you've heard, or one for something you've already done, never comes back.

## A lighter download

The game's art now ships in smaller formats. Paintings are packed much tighter, and pixel art is packed without losing a single pixel. The full download goes from about 337 MB to about 113 MB, with no visible change to the art.

## Hero changes

Each 5★ was measured in test fights against a target for their role, and the heroes below move to meet it. Two of the lower-rarity healers and the element gifts were measured the same way.

### The Arbiter

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contempt of Court | 90% ATK damage, 140% if the target has any debuff | 100% ATK damage, and every debuff on the target lasts 1 more turn |
| Final Verdict | 200% ATK damage ignoring 50% DEF. With 2+ debuffs on the target: 280% and Stun 1 turn | 150% ATK damage ignoring 50% DEF. Uses up every debuff on the target for +40% each (up to 3). Stuns 1 turn if it used up 3 and the hit lands |
| Base ATK | 48 | 40 |

Control effects (Stun, Sleep, Charm, Freeze), debuffs that can't be cleansed or never run out, and leader-skill debuffs are never stretched or used up. Her card lists them.

**Why:** She was above the target for her role, and Contempt of Court almost never got used. The Sentence gives it a job, and at 40 ATK she lands on the target.
{: .why}

### Hollow Kiln

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the Kiln | Needs 30 Essence. 80% ATK damage + 2% per Essence to all enemies, Burn, and The Cinder gains Inferno | Needs 40 Essence. 2% ATK damage per Essence to all enemies (100% at 50), Burn |
| The Cinder: Tethered (new) | | When The Cinder loses HP to a hit, Hollow Kiln loses the same amount. Her DEF doesn't lower it, and it can kill her |
| Base ATK | 42 | 34 |

**Why:** She was well above the target for her role. Together these land her on it.
{: .why}

### Selwyn, Twice Seen

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Other Selwyn: Unseen (new) | | Enemies can't pick her as a target. Area hits still reach her |
| Hindsight | 50% of Selwyn's ATK | 25% of Selwyn's ATK |
| Un-happen It | 100% ATK + 1.8% for each percent of her max HP spent (max 280%) | 100% ATK + 1.2% for each percent (max 200%) |
| Base ATK | 46 | 33 |

**Why:** Much of The Other Selwyn's value was the enemy attacks she drew away. With Unseen she draws none, and the lower numbers land Selwyn on the target for her role.
{: .why}

### Mireha, the Rot Drinker

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mire Touch | Heals 130% ATK, 170% with 2+ debuffs on Mireha | Heals 91% ATK, 119% with 2+ debuffs on Mireha |

Sin Absorption, Dissolution Prayer and her basic attack are never held by Steeping.

### Element gifts

| Gift | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green Mend (Nature) | All allies heal 8% max HP at each wave start | 5% max HP |
| Wellspring (Arcane) | All allies gain 15 of their class resource at each wave start | 10 |

Both sides of a fight get the change, Colosseum and rivals included.

**Why:** These two were well ahead of every other element gift. The new numbers put them in line with the rest.
{: .why}

## Also in this patch

- Easier reading: the fancy italic font is gone. Expeditions, the region list, the intro and Arm Wrestling use the same title font as the rest of the game, and text over a painting gets a dark backing.
- Expedition returns stand your party at the same height on every route. The party bonus gets its own moment, with marks over the heroes who earned it, and collecting one return opens the next straight away.
- The shop's buy panel is easier to read and use: it flashes gold as it opens, the good you tapped lights up on the shelf, − / + / Max are bigger, and Buy is a gold button.
- The parcel page drops the "Next seal" countdown.

## Fixes

- A skill still on cooldown can't be used by mistake.
- The Arbiter's card lists exactly which debuffs her Sentence leaves alone.
