"A place where light goes to die." That's how the game describes the Underground Morass, and this patch takes it at its word. Down there nothing sees, so everything listens. Make a racket and something will come to see about it.

4.10.0 rebuilds two regions. The Morass gets a new map and monsters that hunt by sound. The Gate to Aquaria gets a Kraken that reaches into every pool with its arms, and you can cut them off one at a time before you meet the rest of it.

The Summit, the Blistering Cliffsides and the Caverns cult all learn new tricks. Flick from the Iron Oath will fight beside you if the money's right. And the fire heroes get reworked, so the things in the Morass that come for the loudest hero will have plenty to come for.

## The Underground Morass, rebuilt

The Morass has a new map: twelve nodes, a main road down to the black lake, and three side passages off it.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.morass_rooms media=320 %}

## Hunted by sound

Every hero makes noise, and it depends on what they did last. A heal or a buff is silent. A basic attack is Soft. A damaging skill on one enemy is Sharp, and anything that hits more than one is Loud. Arcane heroes are one level quieter, and a hero in Stealth makes no sound at all.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.noise scale=2 scale_sm=2 caption="Noise, as it shows on a hero's plate" %}

While a hunter stands, rings at each hero's feet show how loud they are, and the skill page tells you how loud a skill is before you cast it. Gloom Stalkers and Blind Horrors go for the loudest hero. So the big area hit that clears the wave also paints a target on whoever threw it.

The rest of the Morass plays along. A Fungal Zombie bursts when it dies, and everyone coughs one level louder. A hero with a Cave Leech on them can't keep still.

## The Blind Matriarch

At the Abyssal Exit, past the last of her children, the mother of the Blind Horrors lies half in the black water.

{% include figure.html src="blind_matriarch.webp" alt="The Blind Matriarch, a huge pale eyeless beast with a wide toothy mouth, crouched low" caption="The Blind Matriarch" scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

> The mother of the Blind Horrors, huge and eyeless, lies half in the black water at the edge of the deep. She has heard every footstep that ever came this far.
>
> The Blind Matriarch, from the Bestiary

She calls her young up out of the water. Sometimes she goes very still and listens. That would be a good time to be quiet.

## Orlo's garden

{% include figure.html src="orlo.webp" alt="An old man with a grey beard full of tiny glowing mushrooms, sitting on a mound of fungus, a finger to his lips" caption="Orlo" side="wide" %}

Off the Spore Cavern, past the spore drifts, a nook glows a steady cold green. There are fungus beds in straight rows, edged with stones. In the back wall a man is growing out of the rock, or the rock out of him. His beard is full of tiny caps and he has a pruning knife in one hand.

He holds a finger to his lips before you've said a word.

> Quietly. Bed four's coming up and it hates a draught.
>
> Orlo

Orlo has lived down here a long time, and he knows exactly how the Morass hunts. He has something to sell you. What you buy is up to you.

## The Gate to Aquaria, rebuilt

"The threshold between earth and abyss." The merfolk hold the Gate with spear and sorcery. Under them, in the deepest water, is the Kraken they keep as their last gatekeeper, and it reaches up through every pool and channel.

{% include figure.html src="gate_map.webp" alt="The new map of the Gate to Aquaria: tidal pools, coral terraces, a stilt village, a wrecked ship held by tentacles, a round flooded hall, a coral wall and a dark pool with the shadow of a giant octopus" caption="The Gate to Aquaria" %}

Seven nodes each have one of the Kraken's arms in them. An arm lets go after three turns and slides back under, and it'll be there again next time. Kill it before it lets go and it's severed for good. Every arm you sever is one fewer that rises with the Kraken at the end.

## Thessaly, at the Warden's Rock

{% include figure.html src="thessaly.webp" alt="A merfolk woman in coral armour sitting on a rock, forearms on her knees, a spiral shell shield and a spear beside her" caption="Thessaly" side="wide" %}

On a flat rock past the Merfolk Outpost's last stilt-hut, a Gatewarden sits with her forearms on her knees. Her shell shield lies face down in the sand. Her spear is stuck upright beside it, and she isn't holding it.

> Forty years on that wall. Thirty of them I walked people down to the deep water when the priests said it was hungry.
>
> Thessaly

She's off duty now. Permanently, as of this morning. She knows things about the Wall and what lives under it, and she'll tell a stranger one of them. You pick which.

## The Summit and the Cliffsides

The Summit and the Blistering Cliffsides get new waves, and their enemies get tricks worth planning around.

On the Summit, the Ancient Titan calls up two Ice Wraiths when it falls to half health. When an Avalanche Marmot dies, the avalanche hits everyone, its own side too. Every hero and every enemy loses 15% of max HP, and every Burn goes out. Pop it while its friends are low.

{% include figure.html src="healing_down.webp" alt="A green heart with a red arrow pointing down" caption="Healing Down" scale=2 scale_sm=2 side=true %}

On the Cliffsides, the enemies carry passives that tell you who to kill first. A Fire Elemental flares at any hero who heals an ally and sets them Burning. While a Magma Golem stands, its Radiant Heat cuts the healing your heroes receive by 30%. A Flame Salamander strikes every Marked hero at the end of its turn.

## The Caverns cult fights as one

The cult in the Echoing Caverns looks after its own now. Every time a cultist falls, the cultists still standing gain a Blood Offering, +10% ATK for the rest of the wave, and it stacks. Kill order matters. Waves past the entrance field at least four, with Pact-Bound guards in front.

## Hire Flick

Flick from the Iron Oath takes contracts now. Beat the Oath once and you can hire him at their camp for 500 gold. He fights in your party for three clears at your heroes' average level, then goes home to rest before he'll sign on again. He rests less once the Oath counts you as allies. You can't challenge the Oath while you've got their alchemist.

## Hero changes

Fire heroes get reworked, Yggra trades a shield for a stun, and most of the rest of the list goes up.

### Shasha Ember Witch

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fireball | 130% of ATK to one enemy, and 50% of ATK to up to 2 other random enemies | 130% of ATK to one enemy, then Burns it and up to 2 other enemies for 40% of ATK a turn for 2 turns. No splash damage |
| Conflagration | Every Burn deals its remaining damage at once, +10% of ATK | Every Burn deals its remaining damage at once, +70% of ATK |

**Why:** Fireball spam out-damaged her whole burn kit, so Conflagration almost never got cast. Fireball now sets the fires and Conflagration cashes them in.
{: .why}

### Crane

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lantern Swing | 70% of ATK and Burns the target for 30% of Crane's ATK for 3 turns | 70% of ATK. A Burning target's Burn lasts 1 more turn (4 at most); anything else gets his Burn as before |
| Torch at the Front | 55% of ATK to all enemies and Burns each for 15% of Crane's ATK for 2 turns | 55% of ATK to all enemies. Each Burning enemy's Burn lasts 1 more turn (4 at most); the rest get his Burn as before |

**Why:** only the strongest Burn on an enemy counts, so his Burns fizzled next to Shasha's. Now he keeps her fires going longer instead.
{: .why}

### Garrow

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Pyre Rite | Any enemy he kills with a fire skill, a strike-back or his own Burn stays dead | Only the Condemned stays dead when he kills it that way |

**Why:** one fire skill could lay a whole wave of rising enemies to rest at once. A counter is meant to weaken a trick, and this one deleted it.
{: .why}

### Yggra, the World Root

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blessing of the World Root | Heals all allies for 55% of ATK, +10% for each poisoned enemy | Heals all allies for 45% of ATK, +10% for each poisoned enemy |
| Bark Shield → Taproot (level 3) | Bark Shield: heals one ally for 50% of ATK and gives them Thorns for 3 turns, 18 MP | Taproot: stuns one enemy for 1 turn, 2 if it is Poisoned. Bosses are unaffected. 20 MP, 3-turn cooldown |
| Nature's Reclamation | 150% of ATK to one enemy, heals all allies for 25% of the damage | 150% of ATK to one enemy, heals the ally with the lowest HP for 50% of the damage |

**Why:** Yggra was overtuned. Bark Shield felt out of place in her kit, and her party heal is still impressive at 45%.
{: .why}

### Fennick

**Why:** in playtesting he basic-attacked three turns in four. Taunting the whole wave at 30% evasion meant nearly every hit took his Focus.

## Also in this patch

- The Kraken has its own fight music, and minibosses get a new track.
- Damage-over-time ticks no longer take a Ranger's Focus.
- Breaking an elemental ward no longer stuns a regular enemy.
- Healing Down gets its own icon.
- Echoing Caverns first clears pay 100 gems, and cultist nodes no longer end on boss music.

## Fixes

- A last killing blow no longer spills a round of MP, Valor and leader effects into the next wave.
- Alchemist Essence now resets to a third at the start of every quest and Hard Mode wave.
- A skill heal that Heal Block stops now says so.
- The gem shop no longer offers an item with no price.
