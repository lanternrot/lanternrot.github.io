"A place where light goes to die." That is how the game describes the Underground Morass, and 4.10.0 takes it at its word. Down in the dark nothing sees, so everything listens. Make a racket and something hungry will come to see about it.

Further along the road, at the Gate to Aquaria, the merfolk have woken their oldest gatekeeper. Long before you reach the deep pool at the end of the hall, the Kraken's arms are already reaching up through the water to meet you.

## The Underground Morass, rebuilt

The Morass has a new map, with a main road winding down to the black lake and three side passages branching off into the dark.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.morass_rooms media=320 %}

## Hunted by sound

Every hero makes noise in the Morass, depending on what they did last. A heal or a buff is silent. A basic attack is Soft. A damaging skill on one enemy is Sharp, and anything that hits a crowd is Loud. Arcane heroes are a step quieter, and a hero in Stealth makes no sound at all.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.noise scale=2 scale_sm=2 caption="Noise, as it shows on a hero's plate" %}

While a blind hunter stands, rings at each hero's feet show how loud they are, and the skill page warns you how much noise a skill will make before you cast it. Gloom Stalkers and Blind Horrors go straight for the loudest hero. That big area hit that clears the wave also paints a target on whoever threw it.

The rest of the Morass plays along. A Fungal Zombie bursts when it dies and makes everyone cough louder, while a hero with a Cave Leech clamped on them thrashes too hard to stay quiet.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.morass_monsters scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

## The Blind Matriarch

At the Abyssal Exit, past the last of her children, the mother of the Blind Horrors lies half in the black water.

{% include figure.html src="blind_matriarch.webp" alt="The Blind Matriarch, a huge pale eyeless beast with a wide toothy mouth, crouched low" caption="The Blind Matriarch" scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

> The mother of the Blind Horrors, huge and eyeless, lies half in the black water at the edge of the deep. She has heard every footstep that ever came this far.
>
> The Blind Matriarch, from the Bestiary

She calls her young up out of the water. Sometimes she goes very still and listens. That would be a good time to be quiet.

## Orlo's garden

{% include figure.html src="orlo.webp" alt="An old man with a grey beard full of tiny glowing mushrooms, sitting on a mound of fungus, a finger to his lips" caption="Orlo" side="wide" %}

Off the Spore Cavern, past the spore drifts, a nook glows a steady cold green. There are fungus beds in straight rows, edged with stones. In the back wall a man is growing out of the rock, or the rock out of him. His beard is full of tiny caps and he has a pruning knife in one hand.

He holds a finger to his lips before you've said a word.

> Quietly. Bed four's coming up and it hates a draught.
>
> Orlo

Orlo has lived down here a long time, and he knows exactly how the Morass hunts. He has something to sell you. What you buy is up to you.

## The Gate to Aquaria, rebuilt

"The threshold between earth and abyss." The merfolk hold the Gate with spear and sorcery. Under them, in the deepest water, is the Kraken they keep as their last gatekeeper, and it reaches up through every pool and channel.

{% include figure.html src="gate_map.webp" alt="The new map of the Gate to Aquaria: tidal pools, coral terraces, a stilt village, a wrecked ship held by tentacles, a round flooded hall, a coral wall and a dark pool with the shadow of a giant octopus" caption="The Gate to Aquaria" %}

Along the road to the deep water, single arms of the Kraken rise out of the pools to drag heroes under. Take too long and an arm lets go and slides back into the dark, waiting for you to return. Kill it before it slips away and it is severed for good, leaving one fewer arm to rise with the Kraken at the end.

## Thessaly, at the Warden's Rock

{% include figure.html src="thessaly.webp" alt="A merfolk woman in coral armour sitting on a rock, forearms on her knees, a spiral shell shield and a spear beside her" caption="Thessaly" side="wide" %}

On a flat rock past the Merfolk Outpost's last stilt-hut, a Gatewarden sits with her forearms on her knees. Her shell shield lies face down in the sand. Her spear is stuck upright beside it, and she isn't holding it.

> Forty years on that wall. Thirty of them I walked people down to the deep water when the priests said it was hungry.
>
> Thessaly

She's off duty now. Permanently, as of this morning. She knows things about the Wall and what lives under it, and she'll tell a stranger one of them. You pick which.

## The Summit and the Cliffsides

The Summit and the Blistering Cliffsides get new waves, and the locals have learned habits worth planning around.

Up on the Summit, the Ancient Titan calls Ice Wraiths to his side once you bloodied him, and when an Avalanche Marmot falls, the snowslide buries both sides and snuffs out every Burn on the field. Time the avalanche while its friends are low.

{% include figure.html src="healing_down.webp" alt="A green heart with a red arrow pointing down" caption="Healing Down" scale=2 scale_sm=2 side=true %}

On the Cliffsides, the monsters tell you who to kill first. Fire Elementals flare at any hero who stops to heal an ally and set the healer Burning. While a Magma Golem stands, its Radiant Heat withers the healing your party receives. And Flame Salamanders lunge after every Marked hero at the end of their turn.

## The Caverns cult fights as one

The cult in the Echoing Caverns looks after its own now. Every time a cultist falls, the survivors drink in a Blood Offering that makes them hit harder for the rest of the wave, with Pact-Bound guards standing up front to buy them time.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.cult scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

## Hire Flick

Flick from the Iron Oath takes contracts now. Beat the Oath once and you can hire their alchemist at camp to fight in your party for a handful of clears before he heads home to rest. Once the Oath counts you as friends, he rests less between jobs. Naturally, you can't challenge the Oath while their alchemist is on your payroll.

## Hero changes

Fire heroes get reworked, Yggra trades a shield for a stun, and most of the rest of the list goes up.

### Shasha Ember Witch

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fireball | 130% of ATK to one enemy, and 50% of ATK to up to 2 other random enemies | 130% of ATK to one enemy, then Burns it and up to 2 other enemies for 40% of ATK a turn for 2 turns. No splash damage |
| Conflagration | Every Burn deals its remaining damage at once, +10% of ATK | Every Burn deals its remaining damage at once, +70% of ATK |

**Why:** Fireball spam out-damaged her whole burn kit, so Conflagration almost never got cast. Fireball now sets the fires and Conflagration cashes them in.
{: .why}

### Crane

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lantern Swing | 70% of ATK and Burns the target for 30% of Crane's ATK for 3 turns | 70% of ATK. A Burning target's Burn lasts 1 more turn (4 at most); anything else gets his Burn as before |
| Torch at the Front | 55% of ATK to all enemies and Burns each for 15% of Crane's ATK for 2 turns | 55% of ATK to all enemies. Each Burning enemy's Burn lasts 1 more turn (4 at most); the rest get his Burn as before |

**Why:** only the strongest Burn on an enemy counts, so his Burns fizzled next to Shasha's. Now he keeps her fires going longer instead.
{: .why}

### Garrow

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Pyre Rite | Any enemy he kills with a fire skill, a strike-back or his own Burn stays dead | Only the Condemned stays dead when he kills it that way |

**Why:** one fire skill could lay a whole wave of rising enemies to rest at once. A counter is meant to weaken a trick, and this one deleted it.
{: .why}

### Yggra, the World Root

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blessing of the World Root | Heals all allies for 55% of ATK, +10% for each poisoned enemy | Heals all allies for 45% of ATK, +10% for each poisoned enemy |
| Bark Shield → Taproot (level 3) | Bark Shield: heals one ally for 50% of ATK and gives them Thorns for 3 turns, 18 MP | Taproot: stuns one enemy for 1 turn, 2 if it is Poisoned. Bosses are unaffected. 20 MP, 3-turn cooldown |
| Nature's Reclamation | 150% of ATK to one enemy, heals all allies for 25% of the damage | 150% of ATK to one enemy, heals the ally with the lowest HP for 50% of the damage |

**Why:** Yggra was overtuned. Bark Shield felt out of place in her kit, and her party heal is still impressive at 45%.
{: .why}

### Fennick

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Come and Get Me | Taunts all enemies and gains 30% evasion for 3 turns | Taunts all enemies and gains 50% evasion for 3 turns |
| Fox's Cunning | 20% evasion for 3 turns | 35% evasion and Provoke for 3 turns: each single-target enemy attack has a 50% chance to be drawn to him |

**Why:** in playtesting he basic-attacked three turns in four. Taunting the whole wave at 30% evasion meant nearly every hit took his Focus.
{: .why}

### Knuckle Bess

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roll With It | +10% ATK and +15% DEF for 2 turns, 20 Rage | +25% ATK and +15% DEF for 4 turns, 15 Rage |

**Why:** in playtesting she never cast it. It didn't feel worth a turn, and a buff on yourself should last a good while.
{: .why}

### Bertan the Gatherer

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Herbal Remedy | Heals one ally for 120% of ATK | Heals one ally for 180% of ATK |
| Herbal Tonic | Regeneration for 45% of ATK a turn for 3 turns | Regeneration for 60% of ATK a turn for 3 turns |
| Forager's Pouch | The most wounded ally gets a 45% Herbal Tonic each wave | The most wounded ally gets a 60% Herbal Tonic each wave |
| Nature's Bounty | Heals one ally for 150% of ATK | Heals one ally for 220% of ATK |

**Why:** in playtesting a 1★, Soot Nan, out-healed him.
{: .why}

### Grandma Helga

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cup of Tea | Regeneration for 25% of ATK a turn for 3 turns | Regeneration for 50% of ATK a turn for 3 turns |

**Why:** at 25% it healed about 13 HP over three turns, less than Bertan's Herbal Tonic. Now it's the cheap heal you set up before the damage lands.
{: .why}

### Penny Dreadful

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Good Silver | ATK Down 15% for 2 turns | ATK Down 20% for 2 turns |
| Poison Cocktail | Damage doesn't change with Volatility | Damage grows with Volatility, like A Spot of Tea |

**Why:** Her support wasn't as strong as it needed to be, and Volatility not touching her skills felt wrong.
{: .why}

### Darl

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twine and Prayer | +25% ATK for 3 turns | +25% ATK for 4 turns |

**Why:** a buff on yourself costs a turn, so it should last long enough to pay that turn back.
{: .why}

### Harl the Handsom

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing Ovation (Finale) | Every ally heals for 15% of Harl's ATK | Every ally heals for 30% of Harl's ATK |

**Why:** Harl isn't a healer, but his Finale's heal should be at least a decent chip.
{: .why}

### Kensin

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retribution | The target must attack Kensin on its next turn | The target can only attack Kensin on its next turn |

**Why:** Provoked never forced an attack. It limits who the enemy can attack, and the text now says so.
{: .why}

### Sorju, Gate Guard

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shut the Gate | Provoked enemies must attack Sorju | Provoked enemies can only attack Sorju |

**Why:** the same text fix as Kensin's. Provoked limits who the enemy can attack and never forced an attack.
{: .why}

## Also in this patch

- The Kraken has its own fight music, and minibosses get a new track.
- Damage-over-time ticks no longer take a Ranger's Focus.
- Breaking an elemental ward no longer stuns a regular enemy.
- Healing Down gets its own icon.
- Echoing Caverns first clears now pay out gems, and cultist nodes no longer end on boss music.

## Fixes

- A last killing blow no longer spills a round of MP, Valor and leader effects into the next wave.
- Alchemist Essence now resets to a third at the start of every quest and Hard Mode wave.
- A skill heal that Heal Block stops now says so.
- The gem shop no longer offers an item with no price.
