lanternrot.com is live. Lantern Rot is an independent game and software studio, and for now the site holds two things.

The first is Dorf, a dark-fantasy hero battler for Android. It is in a private beta, and you can ask for a spot on the [home page](/#beta).

The second is a set of five Twitch chat games, each built for a particular streamer's channel: Cat Cafe, Duckie Pond, Cookie Cafe, Fox Den and Wizard's Grimoire. You can see them [here](/#games).

New Dorf releases and studio news will be posted here as they happen, and in the [RSS feed](/feed.xml).
