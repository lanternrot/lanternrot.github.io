Dorf has always had adventurers. Until now, they were all yours.

4.9.0 puts a second party in the Echoing Caverns. The Iron Oath camps past Warden's Post with a tent, a pot over the fire and a banner with an anvil on it. The knight by the fire would like you to state your business, or move along.

You can fight them, shop at their counter, and slowly get them to like you. They fight back the way your own heroes do, with class meters and a plan. The world map gets a new look too. Every node is a little pixel-art landmark now, and the trails between them are boot prints.

## Meet the Iron Oath

The game sums them up as "a scarred knight, a twitchy alchemist, and a bard who won't shut up." Ser Hagen holds the line with a sword and a battered shield. Flick throws fire for a living. Merry Tel plays the lute, mostly at you.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.oath_paintings cols=3 cols_sm=3 %}

Walk up to their camp and you can challenge them, or trade at their counter. Every win counts. Beat them enough times and the Oath warms to you. The fire gets lit, someone clears you a seat, and the counter starts offering discounts and a few things nobody else sells.

## They fight like heroes

The Iron Oath fights with a plan. Ser Hagen picks out your hardest hitter and calls the target a turn before he acts, and Flick is listening. When you hear the call, you have a turn to do something about it.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.oath_sprites scale=2 scale_sm=1 caption="The Oath in battle" %}

You can see it coming, too. Rival and Colosseum opponents now show their Valor, Verses and Essence under their health bars, the same meters your own knights, bards and alchemists build.

Knock two of them down and the last one standing asks to yield. Accept, and they walk off with their dignity and think better of you for it. Finish it, and you take the full spoils.

## The map grows landmarks

World map nodes are pixel-art landmarks now. A fight is a sword stuck in the dirt, and once you clear it, it turns into a campfire with a bedroll. Events are notice boards, explorations are cave mouths, obstacles are barricades, shrines are standing stones, and a rival camp is a tent.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.landmarks scale=2 scale_sm=2 caption="New map landmarks" %}

The trails between them are boot prints, left by whoever went first.

## Hero changes

Four heroes get their healing and support retuned. Most of it goes up.

### Twice-Burned Hask

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seared Touch | Heals an ally for 80% of ATK | Heals an ally for 200% of ATK |

**Why:** Hask burns himself for every heal (5% of his current HP), so his single-target heal should be one of the biggest in the game.
{: .why}

### Margit

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brine Wash shield | 18% of Margit's max HP | 35% of Margit's max HP |
| Brine Wash cost | 12 Essence | 24 Essence |
| Brine for Blood shield | 2x the HP she pays | 1.5x the HP she pays, rounded down |

**Why:** Brine Wash was too weak to be worth casting, while the free shields from Brine for Blood were stronger than they looked.
{: .why}

### Darl

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twine and Prayer heal | 10% of his max HP | 20% of his max HP |
| Twine and Prayer ATK buff | +10% ATK for 4 turns | +25% ATK for 3 turns |
| Toad Strangler | Hits left when the target falls are lost | Hits left when the target falls go to another enemy |

**Why:** in playtesting Darl swung between too strong and too weak, and by level 12 he felt underwhelming next to other heroes.
{: .why}

### Soot Nan

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm the Hands | Allies heal for 30% of the damage dealt | Allies heal for 50% of the damage dealt |
| Bank the Coals | Heals one ally for 50% of ATK | Heals one ally for 100% of ATK |

**Why:** in playtesting she had to heal every turn to keep anyone alive.
{: .why}
