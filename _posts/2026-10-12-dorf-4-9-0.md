Dorf 4.9.0 puts a rival adventuring party in the Echoing Caverns, and brings a few changes to how battles and the world map look. Here is what changed:

- New in the Echoing Caverns: the Iron Oath, a rival adventuring party camped past Warden's Post. Challenge Ser Hagen, Flick and Merry Tel, trade at their camp counter, and win them over for discounts and a few things only they sell
- Rivals fight like heroes: Hagen calls his next target a turn ahead, and when they're beaten, the last one standing asks to yield. Accept for their respect, or finish it for the full spoils
- Rival and Colosseum opponents show their Valor, Verses and Essence under their health bars
- World map nodes are now pixel-art landmarks, and the trails between them are boot prints
- Hask, Margit, Darl and Soot Nan heal harder
- Settings and the Codex get an iron-and-stone look
- The Blacksmith no longer merges away gear your heroes are wearing, and Replay after a victory is now press-and-hold so collecting loot can't restart the fight

Updates download inside the game, so there is nothing to reinstall. If you aren't in the beta yet, you can [request a spot](/#beta).
