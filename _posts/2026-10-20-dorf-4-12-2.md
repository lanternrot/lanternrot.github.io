"The land of Veros is dying." That's the first thing Dorf tells a new commander, and now you get to watch it happen. The rot rolls in over the Whispering Woods from the east before you've lit a single candle.

Your heroes take it personally. The first time one of them reaches for their biggest skill in a fight, the whole battle stops to look. Their painting rises out of the sprite and fills the screen, and only then does the blow land. A few of them have something to say first.

## Big moves get their moment

Every hero in the game has an attack painting now, 76 in all. The first time a hero casts their big move in a fight, the painting grows out of their sprite, settles in the middle of the screen and drifts slowly across it. Then it lunges off toward the enemy as the blow lands. A tap anywhere skips the wait.

A hero's big move is their level-12 skill. If that skill is a passive, the moment plays on the skill their painting shows instead. Each hero plays it once per fight, and the fight remembers through every wave of a node, so a long fight doesn't keep stopping for it. In Forlorn Hope, each hero plays it once a run.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.big_moves cols=3 cols_sm=2 caption="Six of the new attack paintings, from the six heroes who speak" %}

Six heroes speak before they strike. Aurora, the Shadow King, Yggra, Sir Gallan, Harl and Swift Arrow each have a few lines in their own voice, set on a leather band across the painting, and none of them says the same one twice in a row.

- Aurora: "Everybody behind me, please! Especially you."
- Sir Gallan: "By order of the Crown, this position is closed."
- Harl: "An encore! By popular demand. Mine."
- Yggra: "Death asks. We say later."

The paintings turn up in two more places. When a party leader's skill kicks in, or a Bard's Finale goes off, that hero's attack painting flares up behind them as a tall, pale ghost and fades away.

## Status effects you can follow

Buffs and debuffs used to blink on and off without a word. Now an icon pops in with a flash of its colour when it lands, and the effect's name floats up off the unit, green for a buff and red for a debuff. An area debuff names itself over every unit it hits.

When an effect ends, a buff dims and sinks out of the row, and a debuff goes out in a rising gold sparkle, on your heroes and the enemy alike. The icons beside it slide over to close the gap.

## Stormwind Peaks

The wind here cuts deeper than any blade, and the main road through the Peaks used to end too soon. It has a new stop now. The Broken Sledge sits by a wrecked sledge in the snow, between Storm Plateau and the fork for Lightning Spire and Howling Cliffs, and the trail runs through it. If your party is already past Storm Plateau, the Sledge is open and waiting.

Mind the Nesting Roc's chicks while you're up there. Kill one and its mother will not forgive you. Every chick she loses makes her angrier, and she remembers exactly which hero did it.

The mountain's three worst neighbours have been redrawn, and one of them has learned a new trick.

## Ser Hagen and Rosara, repainted

Ser Hagen of the Iron Oath used to share a face with "Ninety-Nine" Dask, grey hair, grey beard and all. Dask keeps it. Hagen now has black hair going grey at the temples and a short black beard, and he kept his scar. His sprite and portrait match the new painting.

Rosara's painting now wears the colours she wears in battle, gold plate trimmed with plum.

## A sheet for every unit

Long-press any unit in battle to open its sheet, which is drawn in pixel art now. Your heroes' sheets are the inside cover of their book, oxblood leather turned in over a dark lining, with gilt and brass at the edges. Enemies get brushed iron with fangs at the corners.

Effects read by their shape on both. A chip with round ends helps you and is green. A chip with pointed ends hurts you and is red.

An enemy's skill timers on its sheet count right now, too. They used to run a turn behind, and a skill never showed as ready before the enemy used it. The timers count the enemy's own turns, so Ready in 2 turns means two of its turns.

## The rot comes for the Woods

The opening story pages are new. The Whispering Woods map fills the screen, and the rot creeps in over it from the eastern horizon while smoke banks up and curls across the trees. A tap sends a wash of smoke across the page to turn it. Anyone starting a new game sees it first.

The candle that follows has sounds of its own, a torch's whoosh on each tap and the catch of oil when it takes, and the heroes who answer arrive with the summon screen's spotlight sound.

Down at Whisper Lake, fishing has real sounds at last. You'll hear the hook's splash, the line running out, the reel, the landing, the snap, and the fish that got away.

## Hero changes

### Oskar

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Oskar of the Bitter Watch | Oskar. His title, The Bitter Watch, sits under his name |
| Icebound Oath | Gives one ally a Shield equal to 30% of Oskar's max HP for 3 turns. While it holds, attackers gain 1 Frost. 20 MP | The same Shield, then deals 20% of ATK to every enemy, plus 10% for each Frost stack on it, counting up to 3 stacks. 20 MP |

**Why:** A turn Oskar spent shielding an ally was a turn with no damage at all. Now his protecting turn hurts every foe as well, and hurts more on a team that lays down Frost. His title moves under his name, where every hero's title goes.
{: .why}

### Sir Gallan

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Challenge | Cast on himself: forces all enemies to target Sir Gallan for 1 turn. At 50 Valor, +10% DEF. At 100 Valor, 2 turns. 1 turn cooldown | Aimed at one enemy: deals 80% of ATK to it, then forces all enemies to target Sir Gallan for 1 turn. At 50 Valor, +10% DEF. At 100 Valor, 2 turns. 1 turn cooldown |

**Why:** Parties with Sir Gallan as their tank finished fights slower than parties running a second healer in his place. A hit on his taunting turn brings them about level at the Ancient Titan.
{: .why}

### Shasha Ember Witch

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fireball | Deals 130% of ATK to one enemy, then Burns it and up to 2 other enemies for 40% of ATK a turn for 2 turns | Deals 120% of ATK to one enemy. The Burn is unchanged |

**Why:** In early playtests in the Whispering Woods, Fireball and its Burn ended most waves in a round or two. A smaller hit changes the first few fights, and that's enough for now.
{: .why}

### Bertan the Gatherer

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antidote | Removes Poison, Burn and Bleed from one ally | Removes Poison, Burn, Bleed and Wither from one ally |

**Why:** Wither, new in this patch, is a damage over time like the others, and Antidote clears them all.
{: .why}

## Also in this patch

- Fights wait for the battle doors to open before anything moves. The enemies walk in once the doors are open, a boss rises in full view, and the party leader's moment plays as they arrive. Each enemy's health bar shows when it lands. The doors also stay shut until the battlefield has finished drawing.

## Fixes

- Summon Tickets pay for pulls. A Summon Ticket covers a single pull and a Grand Summon Ticket a ten-pull on a normal banner, before any gems are spent, and the pull buttons show the tickets you hold in place of the gem price. The Black Market still takes gems only.
- A party leader's opening effect on enemies, like Iselda's Dead Air, lands in the Maw, Forlorn Hope, the Colosseum and rival fights, where the enemies arrive after the fight begins.
- In the Echoing Caverns, a fallen cultist's Blood Offering shows one popup over each survivor, where it used to show two. The offering itself always counted once.
