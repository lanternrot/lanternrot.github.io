On the night of the Hollow Tide, a young battle-mage walked out after the river and saw her own death waiting on the far bank. She pulled it back before it could finish. That's Selwyn, and in 4.12.1 she finally dresses the part, in a black Order coat with a lantern burning violet at the end of a ferryman's pole. The Other Selwyn is still a step behind her, soaked through.

Down at Whisper Lake, the fish have lost their stopwatch. A fight lasts as long as you can hold on, and when one gets away, you get to watch it go.

## Selwyn, redrawn

Selwyn's first drawing gave her a grey coat, a little paper lantern on her belt, and a light cupped in one hand. Beside the other 5★ heroes she looked a little plain.

Her new look keeps the white streak and the river-green lining. The coat is ink-black now, trimmed in silver, and she carries an iron lantern that burns violet on a tall ferryman's pole. The Other Selwyn wears the same coat, faded and soaked, with bare muddy hands and mist curling at her feet.

Everything is new: her sprite, her portraits, her tale painting, her battle poses, her idle, and her attack, where she turns side-on and throws a violet bolt. Her skills stay as they were.

## Whisper Lake fights you can win

Fights at Whisper Lake no longer run on a clock. Hold to reel while the fish is calm and let go while it thrashes, as before, and take as long as you need.

Letting go has a cost now. Stop reeling while the fish is calm and it swims slowly back out, at a third of the speed you reel it in. Spend 4 seconds off the reel in all and it gets away. The first half second of each calm stretch is free, so a quick reaction costs you nothing.

Each thrash gives you a moment's warning. Just before it hits, the float jerks, a ring flares on the water, and the rim of the Hold button flashes amber. Let go when you see it.

Line upgrades still matter. A fish one point stronger than your line can be landed with care. It thrashes longer and rests less, so let go the moment it starts. A fish two or more points stronger outpulls you on every thrash. Hold on and it snaps the line. Let go and it runs off with it.

## The one that got away

A lost fish gets its own moment on the water before the fishing spots come back. When the line snaps, there's a spark, the frayed line whips back toward you, and the fish dives with your float. When a fish simply gets away, the line goes limp, the float bobs alone, a tail flicks, and a shadow swims off to the deep. The result shows while it plays, and a tap skips it.

Two of the lake's signs are fixed too. The jumping fish used to leap out of the water backwards. It leaps head-first now. The diving bird used to drop into the lake like a triangle and leave a fish sliding off the other way. It's a tern now, and it glides in, folds its wings, dives, and flies off with a fish in its beak.

## Taunts take the blow

Since 4.11.0 the Ancient Titan winds up his biggest blows a turn ahead. He marks a hero as Singled Out, and on his next turn the blow lands on them. Your answers were to heal or shield that hero, finish him first, or stun, freeze, sleep or Silence him in between.

Now a taunt works too. A hero who taunts after the wind-up and before the blow lands takes it in the marked hero's place. Sir Gallan's Challenge is the obvious one. The Singled Out mark moves onto the taunter under a spotlight that reads Takes the blow, so you can see who it will hit. If the taunt runs out before the blow falls, the mark goes back to the hero the Titan first picked.

It gives a tank a real job in the Titan's fight. The Singled Out tooltip and the Titan's sheet spell out the new rule.

## Also in this patch

- In the tavern, hold a plate near the left or right edge of the screen and the room pans that way, so a plate that lands far from the wash trough can still reach it. The closer to the edge, the faster it goes.
- Your party leader sits only in the tavern's Seat of Honor. They no longer also wander the floor, and another hero takes their place there.

## Fixes

- Taking a blessing at the Glowstone Altar in the Echoing Caverns opens the road to Troll Bridge again, so the back half of the Caverns and Stormwind Peaks can be reached. Saves that already took a blessing are freed when they load.
- On Android, music and sounds stop when you turn the screen off or switch away from the app, and come back when you return.
