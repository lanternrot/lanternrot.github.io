The dead in the Ancient Catacombs were laid to rest. They did not stay.

4.8.0 rebuilds the Catacombs from the floor up. Somebody down in the dark is raising the dead as fast as you put them down, and the Lich King at the end of the road has hidden his death in clay jars scattered through the wings.

Further down, through a hole in the crypt floor, waits the Bell Pit and the chapel bell that fell into it. Bring heroes you can spare.

## The Ancient Catacombs, rebuilt

The Catacombs have a new map and new places to die in it. The main road still runs down to the Lich King's throne, while three side passages branch off into wings of their own.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.rooms media=320 %}

Each of those three wings ends with one of the Lich King's phylactery jars, and the map marks which ones are still whole.

## Down in the dark

Three kinds of trouble keep the dead on their feet. Two of them are new, and an old acquaintance has learned a worse habit.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.monsters scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

## The Last Mourner

{% include figure.html src="last_mourner.webp" alt="A tired woman in a wax-stained apron, holding a lit candle and a basket of candles" caption="Hesper, the Last Mourner" side="wide" %}

Off the Wraith Sanctum there is an alcove so full of candles that the stone has gone soft and yellow with old wax. A candlemaker named Hesper kneels among them with a basket at her hip, trimming wicks with a little pair of shears.

Her husband Tobin is buried in the Chapel of Candles, third niche on the left. She comes down every night to light him a few more candles.

> He was frightened of the dark. Wouldn't say so. Slept with the shutter open in January.
>
> Hesper

She knows something down here keeps getting the dead up, Tobin included. She also knows about a box of grave goods in the chapel porch that nobody comes for any more. She'll let you have it. All she asks is that you leave her candles burning.

That is the choice in front of you. We'll let her tell you the rest.

## Old Knell, at the bottom of the Bell Pit

Old Knell is the newest Genus Loci, and it is a bell. It fell through every floor of the fort above and came to rest in the dark, deep under the Catacombs. It has rung for every death since.

{% include figure.html src="old_knell.webp" alt="Old Knell, a cracked green bronze bell lying in rubble" caption="Old Knell, the Fallen Bell" scale=2 scale_sm=1 %}

We won't tell you what happens when you hit it. You'll hit it anyway.

## A new fire in the Whispering Woods

{% include figure.html src="cinder_dryad.webp" alt="The Cinder Dryad, a charred tree woman with glowing cracks, holding a flame" caption="Cinder Dryad" scale=2 scale_sm=2 side=true %}

The Whispering Woods have a new enemy. The Cinder Dryad is still smoldering, and her touch sets heroes Burning.

> Her bark is still smoldering from the night something set the wood alight. She never went out. Now she walks the trails looking for someone to share it with.
>
> The Cinder Dryad, from the Bestiary

## Hero changes

### Margit

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brine Wash | Unlocks at level 3 | Unlocks at level 1 |
| Brine for Blood (passive) | Unlocks at level 1 | Unlocks at level 3 |

**Why:** at levels 1 and 2, Cold Lancet built up Essence that Margit had nothing to spend on. Brine Wash costs Essence, so it comes first now.
{: .why}

### Darl

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twine and Prayer | +10% ATK for 2 turns | +10% ATK for 4 turns |

**Why:** a buff a hero casts on himself should last a while, about 4 turns.
{: .why}

### Torga Bloodbeat

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finisher's name | Death Knell | Downbeat |

**Why:** the name clashed with Knell, Old Knell's new status. The skill itself works the same.
{: .why}
