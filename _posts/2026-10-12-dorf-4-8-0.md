The dead in the Ancient Catacombs were laid to rest. They did not stay.

4.8.0 rebuilds the Catacombs from the floor up. There is a new map, new rooms, and a Lich King who keeps getting back up while his jars hold. Somebody down here is raising the dead, and it falls to you to make them stop.

Further down there is the Bell Pit, and at the bottom of it waits Old Knell, the chapel bell that fell. Bring heroes you can spare.

## The Ancient Catacombs, rebuilt

The Catacombs have a new map and new places to die in it. The Deep Ossuary, the Embalmers' Hall and the Chapel of Candles each get a battleground of their own.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.rooms cols=3 cols_sm=3 %}

The Lich King has three phylactery jars, and they keep him rising. Necromancers raise the fallen, so a body on the floor is a problem waiting for its turn. Vigil Lights drift through the halls as well.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.catacombs_foes scale=2 scale_sm=1 caption="Catacombs foes" %}

{% include figure.html src="last_mourner.webp" alt="A tired woman in a wax-stained apron, holding a lit candle and a basket of candles" caption="The Last Mourner" side=true %}

> A mourner's candle that never went out. Its light finds the dead wherever they walk and wants them back in their niches, whatever it takes.
>
> The Vigil Light, from the Bestiary

Somewhere in the dark there is also a mourner with a choice to make. She carries a lot of candles.

## Old Knell, at the bottom of the Bell Pit

Old Knell is the newest Genus Loci, and it is a bell. It fell through every floor of the fort above and came to rest in the dark, deep under the Catacombs. It has rung for every death since.

{% include figure.html src="old_knell.webp" alt="Old Knell, a cracked green bronze bell lying in rubble" caption="Old Knell, the Fallen Bell" scale=2 scale_sm=1 %}

We won't tell you what happens when you hit it. You'll hit it anyway.

## A new fire in the Whispering Woods

{% include figure.html src="cinder_dryad.webp" alt="The Cinder Dryad, a charred tree woman with glowing cracks, holding a flame" caption="Cinder Dryad" scale=2 scale_sm=2 side=true %}

The Whispering Woods have a new enemy. The Cinder Dryad is still smoldering, and her touch sets heroes Burning.

> Her bark is still smoldering from the night something set the wood alight. She never went out. Now she walks the trails looking for someone to share it with.
>
> The Cinder Dryad, from the Bestiary

## Every region gets a painting

The region list is repainted. Every region has its own painted card now, and there is a new realm picker where Western Veros and Aquarias get paintings of their own.

## Also in this patch

- The hero roster's filters are down to Sort and More, with counts on every choice.
- The Maw is retuned, and each tier shows the hero level it's meant for.
- Margit gets Brine Wash at level 1, and Darl's Twine and Prayer lasts 4 turns.
- Damage numbers are easier to read.

## Fixes

- Tavern games no longer freeze when left open past midnight.
