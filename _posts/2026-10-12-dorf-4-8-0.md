Dorf 4.8.0 rebuilds the Ancient Catacombs and adds a new thing waiting at the bottom of the Bell Pit. Here is what changed:

- The Ancient Catacombs are rebuilt: a new map, the Lich King's three phylactery jars that keep him rising, Necromancers raising the fallen, Vigil Lights, and a mourner with a choice to make
- New Genus Loci: Old Knell, the fallen chapel bell, waits at the bottom of the Bell Pit, deep under the Catacombs
- New Whispering Woods enemy: the Cinder Dryad, who sets heroes Burning
- The region list is repainted, with a painted card for every region and a realm picker
- The hero roster's filters are simpler: Sort and More, with counts on every choice
- The Maw is retuned, and each tier shows the hero level it's meant for
- Margit gets Brine Wash at level 1, and Darl's Twine and Prayer lasts 4 turns
- Fixes tavern games freezing when left open past midnight, and damage numbers are easier to read

Updates download inside the game, so there is nothing to reinstall. If you aren't in the beta yet, you can [request a spot](/#beta).
