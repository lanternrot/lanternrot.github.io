Setting a goblin on fire used to look like setting a goblin on fire and then reading about it. A small icon by its health bar said Burn, the number came off once a turn, and the goblin stood there looking fine.

Not any more. Fire and poison show on the body now, for as long as they last. You can tell who's cooking from across the field.

## Burning enemies burn

A burning enemy glows orange from its own pixels, and embers drift up off it. Each time the Burn ticks, the fire flares. When the Burn runs out, is used up, or the enemy dies, the fire goes out with a small puff of smoke.

{% include video.html src="burn.mp4" poster="burn_poster.webp" alt="Shasha's Fireball lands on a dire wolf and two goblins: each glows orange with embers rising, flares as its Burn ticks, and goes out with a puff of smoke" caption="Shasha Ember Witch's Fireball, at half speed" %}

The fire waits for the hit, so a Fireball in the air doesn't light anyone before it lands. Reduce Motion keeps the glow and holds it still, with no embers and no flare.

## Poisoned enemies bubble

A poisoned enemy pulses a sickly green, and lime bubbles rise off it and pop. Each tick, the poison flares with a burst of bubbles. When it runs out, it goes out with the same puff.

{% include video.html src="poison.mp4" poster="poison_poster.webp" alt="Veska's Sewer Gas poisons a dire wolf and two goblins: lime bubbles rise and pop off each, the wolf flares lime on its tick, and later the poison goes out with a puff" caption="Veska's Sewer Gas, at half speed" %}

Poison is lime everywhere now: its icon, its damage numbers and the flash on each tick. It used to be purple, close to Wither's.

A foe that is both burning and poisoned shows only one of them: whichever hurts more each turn, by the same number its long-press sheet shows. Three small Poisons add up, so they can outshine one big Burn. When the stronger one takes over, the other fades out quietly. A puff only ever means an effect actually ended.

## Knives and plates

Salia throws her own knife now, point first, and it glints when it lands. Wilf Hobday throws one of his plates, flat with a wobble, and it smashes to pieces on the target. Both are lifted pixel for pixel from their own attack art, so the knife in the air is the knife in her hand.

{% include video.html src="throws.mp4" poster="throws_poster.webp" alt="In the Spider Nest, Salia throws her knife, Wilf Hobday a plate that smashes on a goblin, and Swift Arrow shoots an arrow" caption="Salia's knife, Wilf's plate and Swift Arrow's arrow" side=true %}

Fennick and Shinobi Jin don't shoot arrows any more. They step in and strike, the way their attack art always showed them doing.

Skills that only help your own side now look like help. Harl's songs, Dragan's knots, Cousin Simnel's feeds, Salia's But Not Out and every other skill that hurts no enemy now plays its class's buff look.

## Also in this patch

- A foe that hit one of your heroes shows a cracked shield over it for that hero's whole turn, when one of the hero's skills would pay off on it. That covers Fennick's Low Blow and Pin Down, Oskar's Watchman's Strike and The Grateful Dead's Grave Tap
- The Summit's fights are level 25 now, and replays there pay a little more
- The Hibernation Den gets meaner. Harpies move into the Bone Midden, and the Troll Chieftain's Cave ends with a third troll

## Fixes

- A Ranger no longer gets Focus from a heal that Heal Block stopped. A buff from the same skill still gives it
- Quorra's hold preview over a foe shows just the held skill's name
- Ambrose's battle plate fits on small phones
