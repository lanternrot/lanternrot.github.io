Some monsters are made of what they wield. The Magma Golem is molten rock with a grudge. The Cinder Dryad never went out after the night the woods burned. Hit elementals like these with the right element and they change where they stand, and whatever they're made of spills out across the fight.

And a hero you fuse now grows into every star they gain.

## Elemental reactions

Hit the Magma Golem with Frost and its lava crusts over, so its Molten Slam comes a turn later. The crust steams, though, and the steam gives every monster on its side Evasion for a round. You bought time and paid for it in cover.

Every reaction works that way. One side is what you'd guess from what the elemental is made of. The other is what spills over, and the spill is rarely on your side. There are eleven elementals so far, and each one spills one of five things onto the fight.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.products scale=2 scale_sm=2 caption="Steam, a blast of fire, meltwater, coral shards and dust" %}

Steam is cover. A blast catches the whole party. Meltwater puts fires out, whoever lit them. Shards go looking for someone to hit, and dust makes everyone on the field hard to see.

You'll know when it happens. The hit lands, and a beat later the elemental changes where it stands while the spill washes across the field with a hiss and a crack. A short line over the elemental says what it just lost, and when that loss bites later, the fight tells you again.

You don't have to guess blind. Long-press an elemental in battle and its sheet lists what it reacts to, from the first time you meet it. The first Cinder Dryad you meet brings a tip of her own.

> Some creatures are made of what they wield, and they react when the right element hits them.
>
> The Elemental Beings tip

Seven more are out there. Look at what the elementals are made of, and decide what might make it react.

## Fusion

Every star a hero gains now adds 15% to their HP, ATK and DEF, and each star builds on the one before. A 1★ fused all the way to 5★ comes out with 5★-sized stats. Heroes you've already fused get the new numbers as soon as you update, with nothing to redo. Heroes you haven't fused don't change at all.

| Per star | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HP, ATK and DEF | +1 each | +15% each, and each star builds on the last |
| SPD | +1 | +1 |
| MP | +1 | +1, only for heroes who use MP |

Attunement points are added on top. The Field Guide's Fusion page says what a star adds, and the fusion preview and the Rank Up scene show the new numbers.

Here is Darl, the 1★ berserker, fused to 5★ at level 30:

| Darl, 5★, level 30 | 4.14.1 | 4.15.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HP | 143 | 236 |
| ATK | 65 | 101 |
| DEF | 30 | 40 |

**Why:** If someone loves Darl, who am I to say they aren't viable? A hero you've fused to 5★ should stand beside the 5★s of their job, with strengths and weaknesses of their own instead of obvious gaps.

## Hero changes

Fused heroes now land among the native 5★s of their job, so those 5★s set the bar. In test fights, each 5★ was measured against a target for their role, and these three move to meet it.

### Mara Thornheart

| Stat | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base ATK | 52 | 41 |

**Why:** She was above the target for her role. At 41 ATK she lands on it.
{: .why}

### The Shadow King

| Stat | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base HP | 110 | 81 |
| Base ATK | 55 | 41 |

**Why:** He was above the target for his role in every region tested. Taking about a quarter off his HP and ATK brings him close, still a touch above.
{: .why}

### Iselda, Breath of the Glacier

| Stat | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base ATK | 48 | 63 |

**Why:** She was below the target for her role. At 63 ATK she lands on it.
{: .why}

## Also in this patch

- The Maw: a run still going at midnight is no longer wiped. It carries on, and pays the day's best take when it ends. Your party is set for the whole run, and the rest bonus only counts the days you actually skipped.
- Hard mode: replaying a Hard fight stays on Hard, the combat log marks Hard fights, and leaving one opens the map where you fought.
- On phones, the back button does what the on-screen back arrow does. Nothing hides under the bottom bar on Samsung and other Android 15 phones, and Coin Toss fits short screens.
- If your phone's Android System WebView is out of date, the splash screen says so and points you to the Play Store to update it.
- Error reports now also say why Android closed the game, how far the game got as it started, which fight was open if it closed mid-fight, and which browser features your phone's WebView is missing. It helps track down crashes. The same "Send error reports automatically" switch in Settings turns it off.

## Fixes

- Every win now counts toward expeditions, including Genus Loci, the Colosseum, rivals, Forlorn Hope and the Maw.
- A Bard's Finale shows on their Skills page and on the long-press sheet.
- Star ratings are back on the Party page.
- The Bestiary lists the Debt Collectors and the Ringer.
- Heroes from the Colosseum open their Field Guide pages right away.
- Forlorn Hope's battle music stops after a loss.
- A Forlorn Hope team with no leader fights without one.
- Phones with an old WebView get through the intro.
