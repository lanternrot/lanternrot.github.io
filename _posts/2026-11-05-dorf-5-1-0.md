A place where light goes to die. The things that live in the Underground Morass stopped needing it long ago. They've had plenty of time to get good at listening for whatever comes down the tunnel with a sword and a Bard.

Your party has never been quiet in its life.

## The Morass is listening

Everything your heroes do makes a sound now. A heal, a buff or a shield is Silent. A basic attack is Soft. A skill that hits one enemy, or any Bard song, is Sharp. A skill that hits more than one enemy, or a Finale, is Loud.

Monsters with an ear on their plate strike the moment they hear a sound they care about, and they strike whoever made it. A Taunt won't pull that blow away. Once a monster has answered, it's busy until its own turn, and that gap is when you do your damage.

Before you commit to a skill, a mark on a monster's plate tells you it will hear you.

### Down a side tunnel

Between the Damp Tunnels and the Fungal Grotto, a low arch breathes warm air into the wet cold, and something inside it gives off a steady yellow light. That's The Clutch. Someone got there before you, a long time ago.

Orlo still keeps his garden down here, and he has new wares for sale. His advice on using them is short and worth hearing.

## The Ancient Catacombs

The dead here were laid to rest. They did not stay.

The Lich King sits alone on his throne now. He calls his court down around him as he weakens, and the ones you fell do not stay down. Every fight in the Catacombs has new waves.

## The Janxier Floodplain

After the rains, nothing stays buried. The gators have learned to smell blood, and their Death Roll goes straight for your Bleeding heroes. The Hydra's heads go for whoever is hurt the worst. Two new things have moved in beside them.

## Pointers on the map

{% include figure.html src="map_pointers.webp" side=true alt="The Echoing Caverns map at Deep Chasm, with round pointers around the screen's edge, one of them stacked with a count of 3" caption="Pointers on the Echoing Caverns map" %}

Pointers on the map's edge aim at off-screen places worth going to. That covers fights you haven't beaten, the road on and the way back, a Genus Loci, a dark shrine, an idle route, and news from a rival camp. Several in the same direction stack up with a count.

Tap one and the camera glides there. Once a place is on screen, its pointer clears. They work on the world map and on a season's map.

## Hero changes

### Kensin

| Skill | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stand and Fight | Taunt all enemies for 2 turns (3 at 50 Valor, 4 at 100). 15% damage reduction at 75 Valor | Same taunt, and 15% damage reduction while it lasts, at any Valor |
| Retribution | 100% ATK to one enemy (up to 180% at 100 Valor). It is Provoked | Same damage. The target and every enemy of its kind are Provoked. Until his next turn, he counters each of them that hits him for 80% ATK (100% at 50 Valor) |
| Reinforce | Removes ATK and DEF debuffs from an ally and heals 10% of Kensin's DEF (15% at 75 Valor). At 100 Valor, also removes SPD debuffs | Removes ATK and DEF debuffs from an ally and heals them for 8% of Kensin's max HP (12% at 75 Valor). With nothing to remove, they gain DEF Up 20% for 2 turns instead. At 100 Valor, also removes SPD debuffs |
| Level 6 skill | Riposte: counter for 80% ATK for 2 turns (100% at 50 Valor, 3 turns at 75) | The Old Post, a passive: +5% DEF for each enemy taunted or Provoked onto Kensin, up to +20%. A Provoked enemy counts until his next turn |
| Capstone | Braced: Stand and Fight's damage reduction works at any Valor | Nineteen Years: each counter Kensin lands heals him for 3% of his max HP |

**Why:** Kensin was below the target for his role, and on too many fights a party did better with no tank at all. Tanking helps least against swarms, so swarms are his job now.
