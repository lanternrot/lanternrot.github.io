A civilization drowned but not destroyed. Past the reef, Aquaria's people are still at their posts, still on the wall, still on their ships. They have been at it long enough to have a system. Knock one of them down and somebody else picks up his job, his shield or his gun, and carries on as if nothing happened.

Three regions of the sea floor are rebuilt around that. Further out, the patrol's sharks have learned to follow the blood, and they aren't picky about whose it is.

## The Tidewall Ruins, rebuilt

The Tidewall is a city again, and a busy one. Merfolk walk its streets, boats are poled along its canals and fish drift through what used to be the harbour. The road runs eleven fights across a new map, from the barred gate out of the Coral Depths to the Sealed Gate on the far side.

### The Wall

{% include video.html src="shield.mp4" poster="shield_poster.webp" alt="At the Breach, Amyas Merestone fells the Corroded Sentinel, and the Wall Guard next in line takes up the shield on his turn" caption="The shield passes down the line at the Breach" side="wide" %}

The Wall still keeps its watch, and it keeps it in order. Its shield-bearer stands in front and makes everyone look at him. Knock him down and the next guard in line takes the shield up on his turn. Every guard knows his place, and a number on his plate says where it is.

> Its post was a stretch of parapet and a list of who stands behind whom. The parapet is underwater now; the list is not.

Get to the bottom of the list and there is nobody left to hand the shield to. The last of them doesn't take that well.

At the Sealed Gate the whole Wall stands together, and while the shield is raised the guards behind it shrug off a good deal of anything that hits them all at once.

The sea's wildlife still picks through the streets between the Wall's patrols, and the Tide Lurker has a new look to go with it.

### Ione

{% include figure.html src="ione.webp" side="wide" alt="Ione, a green merfolk woman in a striped red and gold headscarf and an apron, arms flung wide behind a counter of jars, shells, a key and a fat old book" caption="Ione" %}

Down a side alley off Low Street, one stall still has its awning up, striped red and gold, the only bright thing on the street. The merfolk woman behind it sees you and stands up so fast her stool goes over.

> A customer! From outside! Nobody's bought so much as a shell off me since the Wall started leaking.

Ione sells Knowledge Tomes and the Ancient Codex, as many as you can pay for, and sea food for the tavern's kitchen: Brine Kelp and Pearl Oysters. She'd love to make a sale. Whether Low Street lets her is another matter.

## The Outer Currents, rebuilt

{% include figure.html src="outer_currents.webp" alt="The Outer Currents' new map: currents running across the sea floor under Aquaria's wall, past a patrol hut, picket stakes and a broken gate, a plaza with a statue and banners, tunnel mouths, round shark pens, a stone barracks, a cluster of glowing buoys and a wreck" caption="The Outer Currents' new map" %}

The sea here has its own will. The Outer Currents run eleven fights across a new map, from the Tidewall's culvert out past the patrol's barracks to Shipyard Junction.

{% include video.html src="shark.mp4" poster="shark_poster.webp" alt="At Patrol Crossing, Salia cuts an Aquarian Enforcer open, and both Patrol Sharks turn and bite him instead of the party" caption="Salia opens an Enforcer up, and the sharks forget all about her" side="wide" %}

The patrol keeps trained sharks, and the sharks follow the blood. They go for whoever is Bleeding, on either side. That includes the merfolk who feed them, though a shark never bites another of its own kind. Their handlers know it and use it.

The Aquarian Enforcers carry barbed tridents, and what they leave in a hero's side doesn't stay a scratch for long. The merfolk have old wounds of their own under the bandages. Current Mages steer the water lanes, and when one of their own is bleeding they turn the current and throw the scent onto your heroes instead.

> Elite soldiers of the sunken city, armed with heavy barbed tridents. They pin intruders in the silt until the wounds tear open and the patrol's sharks arrive.

{% include figure.html src="aquarian_enforcer.webp" scale=2 scale_sm=2 alt="The Aquarian Enforcer's new look: a merfolk soldier in coral-trimmed bronze scale, a red crest, an octopus shield and a barbed trident" caption="The Aquarian Enforcer's new look" %}

Someone waits behind the shark pens.

## The Sunken Shipyard gets its crew

Ghost vessels still creak at their moorings, and the Flagship's crew is still aboard. Every one of them has a job, and the jobs outlive the men. When a crewman falls, the next plain sailor takes his job up and carries on with it.

{% include art-rows.html items=page.art.crew scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

### The swivel gun

{% include video.html src="swivel.mp4" poster="swivel_poster.webp" alt="At the Hull Graveyard, the Bramleigh Hog fells the Gunner, Cousin Simnel fires the empty swivel gun across the crew, a Wreck Scavenger spikes it, and a Drowned Sailor takes up the Gunner's job" caption="The Gunner falls, and Cousin Simnel gets to the gun first" side="wide" %}

The gun belongs to whoever is standing at it. While a crewman holds the Gunner's job, it fires on your party. Knock him down and the job stands empty until the next sailor takes it up on his turn. While nobody mans it, a Fire ribbon turns up on your hero's book, and your heroes can fire it themselves across the whole crew once it's ready.

The crew would rather you didn't. A sailor will be along for the job soon enough, and the Wreck Scavengers know how to spike a gun.

### The Drowned Captain

{% include figure.html src="drowned_captain.webp" side=true scale=2 scale_sm=2 alt="The Drowned Captain: a dead sea captain in a tricorn hat and a rotted coat, trailing weed and mist" caption="The Drowned Captain" %}

The Flagship Wreck is the captain's, and he gives the orders. His crew carries them out.

> He went down with the Flagship and never gave up the deck. He still calls the watch, and the dead still answer.

He has also noticed who has been firing his gun.

## The Old Fort's stair

The dungeon stair under Old Fort Calindash has its bosses now, one for each side that can hold the fort. When the living hold it, the Sergeant waits at the bottom. When the dead hold it, the Castellan does.

## Bramleigh's Hard pays double

Bramleigh's Hard pays twice the Tallies Normal does now, and the season's shop has grown so you can spend them all.

{% include art-strip.html items=page.art.hard_shop scale=2 scale_sm=2 %}

- **The Harvest Plait**, for the tavern wall: "Wheat plaited by someone who meant it, hung where the table can see it." While it hangs, whatever meal is on the table gives double its bonus.
- **The Groaning Board Pie**, a feast dish for XP, and its recipe is sold nowhere else: "Boar, honey and berries under one crust. The board has been groaning for six years."
- Blackiron Ingots for the Blacksmith, and more large and epic tomes.

Cleared Hard fights before the change? Wilf Hobday has been over the tally on the shed wall, and a letter with the difference is waiting in your mailbox.
